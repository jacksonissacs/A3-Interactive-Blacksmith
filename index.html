How to play

  1. Enter a positive whole number and choose Add heat. The forge holds up to 100 heat.
  2. Choose Make sword to spend 30 heat and craft one sword.
  3. Choose Reset forge to start over with 20 heat and zero swords.

Know your forge

  • 0–29Too cold to craft.
  • 30–69Ready to forge.
  • 70–100Roaring fire. Keep crafting!
